RIVAL PARTY

APP. C: RANDOM ENCOUNTERS

Sets character levels and follower type (men-at-arms to level 3, henchmen from level 4).

Encounter Rules Applied:

  • DMG p.175: 2-5 characters (d4+1), rounded out to 9 with followers.
  • Classes by the Character Subtable d%, with the per-party maximums; incompatible results are ignored.
  • Level: dungeon level through 4th, then d6+6 adjusted by 1 toward the dungeon level (max 12 until dungeon level 16).
  • Dungeon level 1-3: men-at-arms (4-7 hp). Level 4+: henchmen at one-third master level, +1 per 3 levels above 8th.
  • About 20% non-human, half of those multi-classed (DMG p.176). Magic items from DMG Tables I-IV.
  • House rule: monster level is taken as the dungeon level.

MERCENARIES

HIRING TABLES: 1E DMG

Rules Applied:

  • Regular troops: level 0 men-at-arms, 4-7 hp each (DMG p.30).
  • Over 5 troops: serjeants, 1 per 10 minimum, 1 per 5 maximum, counted among the troops.
  • Lieutenant (2nd or 3rd level) if more than 10 troops in Tier III or IV.
  • Captain (5th-8th level) if Tier IV has more than 20 troops.
  • Serjeant pay = 10x troop rate. Lt. and Capt. = 100gp per level.
  • Types seldom mixed: generate separate units.
